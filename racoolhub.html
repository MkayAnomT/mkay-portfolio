RacoolHub

A mobile app that lets people discover nearby Wi-Fi hotspots, buy internet access, connect their device and manage their active plan from one place.

01 · Overview

Getting online, without the guesswork.

Getting reliable, affordable internet in public usually means searching for Wi-Fi manually, guessing at prices and data limits, and fighting vouchers, portals and passwords. Once you're on, it's hard to tell how much data or time is left.

RacoolHub turns all of that into one transactional flow: find a hotspot, choose a plan, pay, connect and stay connected. The guiding principle was that getting online should feel as simple as ordering a ride or paying a bill.

02 · The problem

"I need internet."

The people RacoolHub is designed for are students moving between campus, hostel and library, remote workers who need a stable connection away from home, and anyone whose mobile data is unavailable or too expensive. Each of them needs to know what's nearby, what it costs and what they're getting before they pay.

Find a hotspot nearby

Nearby hotspots appear as a list or on a map, with distance, opening status and a starting price on every card.

Clear plans, clear prices

Three plans, priced up front: pay per GB, a 55 GB monthly bundle, or unlimited data by the hour.

Pay with the total in view

Checkout repeats the hotspot, plan and total before payment, and the amount is always written on the pay button.

03 · Core flow

From hotspot to connected.

The whole product is built around one loop: Find Wi-Fi → Choose a plan → Pay → Connect → Stay connected. Every screen in the flow has one primary action that moves the user to the next step.

Payment success never drops the user back on Home. Its main action is Connect to Wi-Fi, and once connected, the remaining data and expiry are always one tap away.

04 · Product screens

One purchase, one clear system.

Beyond the core flow, the app covers the rest of the experience: the connected home, the map, plans, payment progress, the wallet, activity history and the profile.

05 · States

Every outcome has a next step.

Loading, offline, failed and pending are designed screens, not afterthoughts. Each one uses plain language and gives the user one clear way forward.

06 · Admin dashboard

The operator's side of the network.

Behind the app is a web dashboard for the team running the network. The Overview answers the first questions of the day: how many people are connected, how much revenue came in, and which hotspots need attention.

Each section of the sidebar has its own page, built on the same design system as the mobile app. Status is always an icon plus a word, never colour alone. All figures shown are demo data.

07 · Design process

From one loop to a full product.

01

Understand

Map where getting online breaks down in public: finding a hotspot, trusting the price, connecting, and knowing what is left.

02

Structure

Define the core loop and the screen map around it, from discovery through payment, connection and renewal.

03

Design

Build the screens on a Figma design system with shared tokens, Inter type and reusable components for every state.

04

Validate

Audit the flows, prototype links, visual details and motion, and fix what breaks before handing off.

08 · Key decisions

Small details matter.

The decisions that shaped RacoolHub are mostly about trust: knowing the price, knowing what happens next and knowing what's left.

01
Browse first, sign in at checkout.

People can see nearby hotspots and plans without an account. Sign-in is only asked for at checkout, after they know a hotspot is near them.

02
The amount is always on the button.

The pay button always shows the total, so there is never a surprise between choosing a plan and paying for it.

03
Plan status is always visible.

While a plan is active, Home shows the remaining data and time to expiry, and low data and expiry each get their own state with one recovery action.

04
Notifications are events, not a feed.

Every notification maps to a real change: payment, activation, high usage, expiring, expired or a failure.
